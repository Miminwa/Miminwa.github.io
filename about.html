I'm Miracle Chimamanda Ndubuisi, a Security Analyst working across detection engineering, incident response, digital forensics, application security, cloud infrastructure, and security governance within fintech environments.

My work spans both offensive and defensive security. I conduct security assessments across mobile applications, web platforms, APIs, payment systems, and cloud infrastructure, while also building detection and monitoring capabilities, investigating security incidents, improving access controls, and supporting secure infrastructure design.

My current research interests have grown directly from these operational security challenges. I am particularly interested in detection engineering, cloud and infrastructure security, and empirical security measurement: understanding how security controls perform in real-world environments, where they fail, and how their effectiveness can be evaluated and improved.

My path into cybersecurity is unconventional. I hold a First-Class Honours degree in Physics Education, where my undergraduate research involved quantitative study design, data collection, statistical analysis, and peer-reviewed publication. That research background, combined with hands-on security engineering experience, now shapes how I approach technical security problems: not only asking whether a control exists, but whether it works and how its effectiveness can be measured.

I am interested in graduate research opportunities in applied cybersecurity, particularly work involving detection engineering, cloud and infrastructure security, and empirical security measurement, while continuing to grow my professional practice in security engineering.
